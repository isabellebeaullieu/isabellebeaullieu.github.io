Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy explains how Isabelle Beaullieu ("I," "me") collects and uses information through isabellebeaullieu.com (the "Site"). I am a real estate agent licensed in Louisiana and affiliated with Compass, a licensed real estate broker.

Information I collect

Information you give me. When you submit a form on the Site, I collect what you enter, which may include your name, phone number, email address, buying timeline, whether you are pre-approved or working with an agent, your message, and which property you asked about.

Information collected automatically. The Site does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. Like all websites, the services that deliver it may record basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. The Site is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, and those providers may process this information under their own privacy policies.

How I use your information

  • To respond to your inquiry and schedule showings
  • To send you information about properties or services you asked about
  • To keep records of client communications as required by my brokerage and by law

I do not sell your personal information, and I do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Calls, texts, and emails

When you submit a form, you agree that I may contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. You can ask me to stop contacting you at any time. Reply STOP to a text message, or email me at the address below.

How your information is shared

  • Service providers that help me run the Site and manage inquiries, such as Google (which stores form submissions and sends me email alerts) and GitHub (which hosts the Site)
  • My brokerage, Compass, as needed for supervision, compliance, and your real estate transaction
  • Others involved in your transaction, such as lenders, inspectors, or other agents, only when you ask me to or when it is needed to serve you
  • When required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or to protect rights and safety

How long I keep it

I keep inquiry information as long as needed to serve you and to meet my record-keeping obligations, then delete it.

Your choices

You may ask me to access, correct, or delete the information you have submitted, or to stop contacting you. Email me at the address below and I will respond promptly.

Security

I use reputable service providers and take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The Site is not intended for children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
